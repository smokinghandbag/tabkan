TabKan is installed 🎉

Turn your Chrome tab groups into a visual Kanban board — with notes, tags, to-dos, and saved sessions. Pin it to your toolbar so it's always one click away.

Pin TabKan in two steps

Step 1

Click the puzzle piece icon at the top-right of Chrome to open your extensions.

Step 2

Click the pin next to TabKan so its icon stays in the toolbar.

Don't lose your tabs on restart

If Chrome isn't set to reopen your tabs, closing the browser clears every tab and group — and your TabKan board along with them. Turn this on once:

  1. Open Chrome Settings → On startup (paste chrome://settings/onStartup into the address bar).
  2. Choose “Continue where you left off.”

As a safety net, TabKan also keeps a private auto-snapshot of your workspace and offers to restore it if Chrome ever reopens empty.
